# DECISION LEDGER

## Purpose

Trace major decisions to evidence.

## Chain

```text
Reference / Research / Requirement
        ↓
Principle
        ↓
Decision
        ↓
Implementation
        ↓
Outcome
```

## Record

- input source;
- extracted principle;
- decision;
- implementation path;
- result;
- confidence.

This enables decision archaeology and future learning.
