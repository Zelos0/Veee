# NEGATIVE LEARNINGS

Failures are first-class knowledge.

Record:
- what was tried;
- why it was tried;
- what failed;
- evidence;
- likely cause;
- conditions;
- what to avoid;
- whether the conclusion is certain or tentative.

Examples:
- a reference that looked promising but performed poorly;
- an interaction that hurt usability;
- unnecessary technical complexity;
- an animation that caused performance problems;
- a design pattern that repeatedly felt generic.
