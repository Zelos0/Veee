# ANTI-AI SLOP

## Avoid by default

- generic purple/blue AI gradients;
- excessive glassmorphism;
- endless cards;
- centered hero + three cards;
- random 3D;
- meaningless particle fields;
- excessive pills;
- decorative gradients without purpose;
- generic oversized typography;
- animation everywhere.

## Self-test

Ask:
1. Could this have been generated from a generic SaaS prompt?
2. What makes this specific product unmistakable?
3. Which decisions are evidence-based?
4. Where is the signature?
5. Which effects could be removed?

If the answer to #1 is yes, continue refining.
