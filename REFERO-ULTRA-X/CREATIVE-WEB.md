# CREATIVE WEB RESEARCH

## Purpose

Research high-craft digital experiences when the product benefits from a distinctive experience.

## Technologies to consider

- GSAP
- ScrollTrigger
- WebGL
- React Three Fiber
- shaders
- image distortion
- kinetic typography
- masked reveals
- pinned/scrubbed sections
- horizontal scrolling
- magnetic interactions
- cursor systems
- section morphing

## Selection rule

Technology must solve a communication, interaction, storytelling, or brand problem.

Do not add:
- random 3D;
- particle backgrounds;
- effects without hierarchy;
- animation that harms usability;
- heavy WebGL without fallback.

## Signature interaction framework

For every major effect define:
- user intent;
- trigger;
- target;
- visual response;
- duration;
- easing;
- interruption behavior;
- mobile behavior;
- reduced-motion behavior;
- performance fallback.

## Inspiration rule

Borrow:
- principles;
- pacing;
- composition;
- interaction grammar.

Do not reproduce a site's identity or layout without authorization.
